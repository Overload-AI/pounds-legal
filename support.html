POUNDS is made by Give Me Pounds LLC. This page says how to reach us and how to do the things people ask about most.

Your subscription

The coach and the other AI features are a subscription, sold and billed by Apple. We never see your card and cannot charge, cancel or refund it ourselves.

  • Manage or cancel: in the app, Settings → Manage subscription; or on the iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions → POUNDS. Cancel at least a day before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep the paid features until the period you paid for ends.
  • Refund: ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple decides.
  • Bought it and the app does not show it: Settings → Restore purchases, signed in to the same POUNDS account you bought it under.

Logging workouts, programs, records and body measurements is free and stays free without a subscription.

A copy of your data

Settings → Download my data gives you a file with everything POUNDS holds about you: workouts, programs, measurements, coach conversations and what the coach remembers, with links to your photos.

Deleting your account

Settings → DELETE ACCOUNT removes your account and its data from our servers and from the phone. It cannot be undone. It does not cancel a subscription, because Apple holds that: cancel it first, as above.

Turning the AI features off

Settings → AI features turns the coach, photo analysis and coach notifications off. While it is off nothing you log or upload is sent to an AI provider, and the rest of the app keeps working. You can turn it back on there.
