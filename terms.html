4.1 No Medical Advice

The App is a training-log and information tool. It is not a medical device, and it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Never disregard professional medical advice, and never delay seeking it, because of anything you read in the App. If you think you may have a medical emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number immediately.

4.2 The AI Coach Can Be Wrong

The coach and all other AI features of the App are powered by large language models. Such models:

  • produce output that is probabilistic, not deterministic, and can be factually incorrect;
  • can state incorrect information confidently and persuasively;
  • can misjudge appropriate loads, volumes, progressions, exercise selection, and technique;
  • can misinterpret or misestimate body composition from a photograph; and
  • do not perceive your body, your form, your fatigue, your equipment, or your environment.

The coach does not see you. It reasons only from what you have typed and logged. It cannot observe your technique, tell a hard set from an unsafe one, see that a joint is swollen, or know that you slept three hours. Its confidence in a recommendation carries no information about whether that recommendation is safe for you on the day you receive it.

You must independently evaluate every suggestion the coach makes before acting on it. Do not attempt a load, a movement, or a progression that you are not confident you can execute safely. Company makes no representation or warranty as to the accuracy, appropriateness, or safety of any AI-generated output.

4.3 The Coach's Tone Is Deliberate

The coach is written as a hard-driving drill instructor. It is blunt, sardonic, and unsparing, it mocks excuses, and it is intended to be. That register is a deliberate design choice and a core characteristic of the product you are purchasing — it is not a malfunction, and it is not a reason the App is defective.

You acknowledge and agree that:

(a) you are choosing this style of coaching knowingly, and you may stop using the App at any time if you do not want it;

(b) the coach's remarks about your effort, your consistency, your physique, or your results are motivational persona, not assessment, diagnosis, or professional opinion, and carry no clinical meaning whatsoever;

(c) harshness in the coach's output is not evidence that any factual claim in that output is correct — Sections 4.1 and 4.2 apply to everything the coach says, in whatever tone it says it; and

(d) the coach's output is generated automatically and is not reviewed by any person before you see it. It may occasionally produce a remark that is cruder, more personal, or more pointed than intended. Company does not warrant the tone, taste, or content of any generated output.

If pointed criticism of your body, your effort, or your discipline is likely to be harmful to you, do not use the AI Features. See Sections 4.9 and 4.10.

4.4 Automated Progression — The App Increases Your Prescribed Loads

The App does not merely record your training. It prescribes it, and it raises what it prescribes automatically.

When you complete the prescribed work for an exercise at or under the prescribed exertion cap, the App's progression engine increases the target weight for that exercise in your program for the next session, without asking you and without human review. Increments are fixed by rule and are larger for lower-body barbell movements than for other exercises.

You acknowledge and agree that:

(a) these increases are produced by an algorithm operating on data you enter yourself, including your own subjective rating of how hard a set felt. If you log inaccurately, the App will prescribe inaccurately;

(b) a prescribed increase is not a determination that you are physically ready to lift that weight, and must not be treated as one. It is an arithmetic consequence of your last logged session;

(c) the engine cannot see your form, your recovery, your joints, your sleep, your nutrition, or any injury you have not told it about; and

(d) you remain solely responsible for deciding what you actually lift. You may override, reduce, or ignore any prescribed load at any time, and you should do so whenever the prescription does not match how you feel that day.

Never attempt a prescribed load you are not confident you can complete safely with good technique. The correct response to a prescription that feels wrong is to lower it, not to attempt it because the App displayed it.

4.8 Body-Fat Estimates Are Not Measurements

At your request, the App analyzes a photograph you take and reports an estimate of your body-fat percentage, together with commentary.

This is a visual estimate produced by an artificial-intelligence model from a single photograph. It is not a measurement, not a clinical or diagnostic result, and not equivalent to DXA, hydrostatic weighing, bioelectrical impedance, calipers, or any other method of body-composition assessment.

(a) It is reported as a range, and the range is the result. The App deliberately reports a band rather than a single figure, because a single figure would assert a precision this method does not have. Do not treat the midpoint of that band as your body-fat percentage.

(b) The width of the band is a floor, not a guarantee of accuracy. The band's width is derived from published studies of photograph-based body-composition estimation carried out under controlled laboratory conditions — standardized lighting, fixed camera distance, prescribed clothing and posture. The App analyzes whatever photograph you take, in whatever conditions you take it. Your true error may be, and for a poorly-controlled photograph is likely to be, materially larger than the band displayed.

(c) The band is not a measurement of this App's own accuracy. Company has not validated the App's estimates against any reference method. The stated range describes what the published literature reports about this class of technique in general. No representation is made that any particular estimate is accurate to within the displayed band or at all.

(d) Do not rely on it. You must not use any body-fat estimate, band, or commentary produced by the App as a basis for any medical, nutritional, pharmaceutical, weight-loss, weight-gain, competition, or health decision. If a body-composition figure matters to you, obtain one from a qualified professional using a validated method. Company expressly disclaims all liability arising from your reliance on any body-composition output of the App.

(e) Changes between photographs may be noise. A difference between two estimates may reflect lighting, posture, camera angle, hydration, time of day, or model variance rather than any change in your body.
